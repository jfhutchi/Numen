# Attributions

Every third-party file in this repository, with its source, author, licence and the date it was
added.

`tools/check_licenses.sh` fails CI if any file under `assets/third_party/` is not covered by an
entry below. Assets and their attribution entry go in the **same commit** — never one without the
other.

## Permitted licences

CC0 · MIT · Apache-2.0 · BSD · CC-BY

Not permitted: CC-BY-NC, CC-BY-ND, "free for personal use", or anything whose licence cannot be
verified on the specific asset page in under two minutes. When in doubt it is discarded and a
procedural placeholder is generated instead.

Most art in this project is generated by scripts under `tools/` and lives in
`assets/procedural/` — licence-clean by construction and not listed here.
